# ET Branding

*Working name — the identity is built so the name can change without touching the mark.*

ET Branding is a team of AI agents that builds and runs a brand: strategy, content, design and distribution, working together so a business can launch and grow its voice without a full agency.

## The mark: "Social Brain"

![Primary mark](/_blob/8f3b47f26885c96d56b2f7fd5b3e77ef)

One symbol, three ideas.

- **Brain.** Two hemispheres side by side: the AI thinking behind every post, caption and campaign.
- **Chat.** The left hemisphere ends in a speech-bubble tail, so the brain is also a conversation. The agents talk with the client and the client's brand talks with its audience.
- **Social.** Three nodes linked across both hemispheres make the familiar "share" shape, and a lime dot sits on the top-right corner like a notification badge. Together they stand for posting, sharing and engagement on Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and the rest, without copying any one platform's logo.
- **No letters.** The mark has no initials in it, so it still works after any rename.

## Colour

Ultraviolet (`brand`, #6A2BE0) is the base. It carries the mark, primary actions and links. `spark` lime (#C8F03C) is the counterpoint — electric, confident, used for one moment per screen. `brand-deep` and `brand-soft` extend the purple into a tonal range; `ink` and `surface` are violet-tinted so even the neutrals feel on-brand.

| Role | Token | Rule |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | `brand` | Mark, CTAs, links. Text in brand passes 4.5:1 on `surface` in both themes. |
| Accent | `spark` | Always with `ink` text (14:1). Never white on lime. Never more than one lime element per view. |
| Tonal | `brand-deep`, `brand-soft` | Illustration and the tonal mark. Decorative only — not for body text. |
| Neutrals | `ink`, `muted`, `surface`, `surface-raised`, `hairline` | Text and grounds. |

## Logo variants

| File | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `mark-primary.svg` | Default. Ultraviolet brain + lime notification dot on light grounds (`surface`, white). |
| `mark-tonal.svg` | Expressive contexts: decks, social, merch. Deep-violet left hemisphere, lighter right hemisphere, lime dot. |
| `mark-reverse.svg` | On `ink`, `brand-deep` or photography: white brain + lime dot. |
| `mark-mono.svg` | Single ink (#6A2BE0) for stamps, embossing, one-colour print. |
| `app-icon.svg` | App / favicon / avatar on an Ultraviolet tile. |
| `app-icon-dark.svg` | App icon on the dark `ink` tile. |

**Clear space:** keep a margin equal to the notification dot's diameter on all sides (about ⅙ of the mark's width).
**Minimum size:** 16px digital, 8mm print. Below 24px, prefer `app-icon.svg`.
**Don't:** mirror or rotate the mark, move the chat tail or the lime dot, fill in the share network, recolour it in a social platform's brand colours, add outlines, gradients or shadows, or set the mark on a mid-purple where it vanishes.

## Pairing with a name

When the final name is chosen, set it in **Space Grotesk Bold**, `ink` (or white on dark), tracking −2%, with the cap height matching about 40% of the mark's height, spaced one dot-width to the right of the mark. The mark also stands alone — social avatars and app icons never need the name.

## Type

**Space Grotesk** (display) — geometric with rounded quirks that echo the mark's lobes. **Inter** (text) — clean and readable for product UI and long copy. Both are Google Fonts.

## Voice

Confident, practical, a little electric. We talk about outcomes ("launch", "grow", "show up everywhere"), not about AI for its own sake. Short sentences. No jargon.
